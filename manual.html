O que é Fractured Veil?

Você cria criaturas dimensionais. Cuida delas todo dia, leva elas para lutar, cruza elas entre si — e o que sai disso tem valor de verdade, em cristais que dá para trocar por dinheiro na blockchain Polygon.

O ciclo, em cinco linhas

🔁
É isto o tempo todo
Você choca um ovo e nasce um avatar. Você cuida dele — comida, banho, sono, carinho — e ele ganha XP e sobe de nível. Subindo de nível ele muda de fase, e a fase é a raridade dele. Do jovem em diante ele luta, contra o computador ou contra outra pessoa — só o bebê fica de fora. Dois adultos cruzam e dão um ovo novo, melhor que os pais. E o que você não quiser criar, vende.
Uma coisa que muda tudo: raridade aqui não é sorte. Nenhum avatar nasce Raro ou Lendário — todos nascem Comuns. Raro é quem chegou ao nível 11, Lendário é quem chegou ao 27. O bicho valioso é o bicho criado, e é o seu trabalho que o fez valer.

Seus primeiros dez minutos

#O quêOnde
1Escolher seu nome de jogador — é único no jogo inteiro, ninguém mais pode ter o mesmona entrada
2Invocar seu primeiro avatar. São três invocações na conta inteira, e são de graça — daí em diante os avatares vêm de cruzar ou do mercado🪺 chocadeira
3Dar nome ao avatar — também únicoao nascer
4Alimentar, dar banho, fazer carinho. Cuidar dá XP, e no bebê dá cinco vezes maistela de cuidar
5Jogar um minijogo para levantar o humor e ganhar moedas🎮

No nível 5 ele vira Jovem: já pode batalhar e abre a dificuldade Médio. No 11 vira Adulto, e aí pode cruzar, passa a valer como Raro e abre o Difícil.

O que custa dinheiro de verdade

Nada obriga. Cristais 💎 se compram com POL na rede Polygon, e se resgatam de volta pela mesma cotação: 10 💎 = 1 POL, nos dois sentidos, sempre. Quem nunca comprar nada joga o jogo inteiro com moedas 🪙, que se ganham jogando.

O que os cristais compram: espaços para mais avatares, taxa de listagem no mercado, avatares de outros jogadores e o selo da temporada do PvP. O que eles não compram: força em batalha. Nenhum item do jogo mexe nos atributos de combate — só no custo de jogar.
CHAPTER 0
What is Fractured Veil?

You raise dimensional creatures. You care for them daily, take them into battle, breed them with each other — and what comes out of it has real value, in crystals you can cash out on the Polygon blockchain.

The loop, in five lines

🔁
This is the whole game
You hatch an egg and an avatar is born. You care for it — food, baths, sleep, affection — and it earns XP and levels up. Leveling changes its phase, and the phase is its rarity. From the youth phase on it fights, against the computer or against another player — only the baby sits it out. Two adults breed and produce a new egg, better than its parents. And whatever you don't want to raise, you sell.
One thing that changes everything: rarity here is not luck. No avatar is born Rare or Legendary — every one is born Common. Rare means it reached level 11; Legendary means it reached 27. A valuable creature is a raised creature, and your work is what made it worth something.

Your first ten minutes

#WhatWhere
1Pick your player name — it is unique across the whole game, no one else can have itat sign-up
2Summon your first avatar. You get three summons for the whole account, free — after that, avatars come from breeding or the market🪺 hatchery
3Name your avatar — also uniqueat birth
4Feed it, bathe it, pet it. Care gives XP, and in the baby phase it gives five times as muchcare screen

At level 5 it becomes a Youth: it can already fight, and Medium difficulty opens. At 11 it becomes an Adult, and then it can breed, counts as Rare, and unlocks Hard.

What costs real money

Nothing forces you. Crystals 💎 are bought with POL on the Polygon network and redeemed back at the same rate: 10 💎 = 1 POL, both directions, always. Anyone who never spends a cent plays the entire game on coins 🪙, which you earn by playing.

What crystals buy: slots for more avatars, the market listing fee, other players' avatars, and the PvP season pass. What they do not buy: power in battle. No item in this game touches combat stats — only the cost of playing.

A costura

🧵

Por baixo da aparência, cada avatar carrega genes que decidem os quatro atributos de combate e os passivos de criação — quanto ele come, quanto entristece, quanto recupera dormindo. Filhos herdam dos dois pais, e é por isso que cruzar dois avatares bem escolhidos compensa. Veja Ovos e Família.

The Avatar

Every avatar comes out of its egg with three things settled for good: its color, which decides its element; its seam, which is its weakness; and its DNA, which decides what it is made of. None of that changes later — the rest of its life is whatever you make of it.

Fourteen colors, eight elements. Two colors per element on the wheel, and the two colors outside it — white and black — stand alone with light and dark.

There is a ninth damage type, physical, which is everyone's basic attack. It belongs to no color, and no one is vulnerable to it.

The seam

🧵
Where it cracks
Every avatar is vulnerable to one of the eight elements — its seam. It never lands on an element the avatar resists, so it is always a real hole. It is the counterweight to affinities: the rarer the creature, the more it shrugs off, but the seam stays there for anyone who knows to look.

DNA

Under the surface, every avatar carries genes that decide its four combat attributes and its care traits — how fast it gets hungry, how easily it gets sad, how much it recovers while asleep. Offspring inherit from both parents, which is why breeding two well-chosen avatars pays off. See Eggs & Family.

Names

Your player name is up to 18 characters and unique across the whole game — if someone already has it, you pick another. Each avatar's name is unique too. Case and accents don't count for the comparison: Náyade and nayade are the same name.

Every meal fills an invisible pressure gauge by 30 to 40 points. At 100, poop appears on screen. Picking it up gives bond; letting it pile up costs 0.1 mood per cycle on top of the hygiene already draining.

Um Lendário de fogo que cura com fogo parece imbatível até alguém olhar a costura dele e trazer o elemento certo. É para isso que serve o Examinar em combate.
CHAPTER 4
The Sheet

Combat runs on the Fabula Ultima engine. The difference that changes everything: here an attribute is not a number, it is a die. Vigor 10 means its vigor rolls a d10.

The four attributes

Each one is a die between d6 and d12, in steps of two: d6, d8, d10, d12. There is no d7 or d9 — raising an attribute means moving one step up that list, and d12 is the ceiling.

Every avatar is born with its four dice arranged one of these ways:

The Legendary +80 HP is the largest single power jump in the game, and it arrives with the second rarity step. An elder is not just an adult with more levels: it takes nearly twice the punishment.

Affinities

Every avatar resists, absorbs, or suffers more from certain elements. This scales with rarity, which is the phase:

RarityWhat it gets

Weighing against that is the seam, its vulnerability to one element. When rules collide, the order is: absorb beats everything; then immunity; resisting and being vulnerable at the same time cancel out.

A Legendary fire creature that heals on fire looks unbeatable until someone reads its seam and brings the right element. That is what Study is for in combat.

CHAPTER 13
Minigames

Three games, played by whichever avatar is open on the care screen. They lift its mood, pay coins and XP, and are the fastest way to get a baby off the ground.

The three

🐍
Elemental Snake
The classic snake. Difficulty changes the speed and the food target: from 10 on Easy to 30 on Master. On Master the snake moves three times faster than on Easy.
🔮
Orb Fusion
Matching orbs that touch merge into the next one up, across twelve tiers. Difficulty changes the point target — 3,800 on Easy, 6,800 on Master — and how bombs appear.
🧱
Elemental Tetra
Falling pieces, completed lines. Difficulty changes the fall speed and the line target: 20 on Easy, 32 on Master. And every ten lines the pieces fall 18% faster.
